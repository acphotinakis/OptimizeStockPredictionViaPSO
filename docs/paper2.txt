Abstract
The stock index reflects the development of national economy and is
a dynamic, complex, nonlinear system. Accurately predicting future
trends in the stock market has important practical significance
for regulators, investment institutions, and individuals. With the
rapid development of machine learning and deep learning, using
time series models, neural networks, machine learning algorithms
and other models to predict future trends in the stock market has
become a hot research topic. Although Long Short-Term Memory
(LSTM) network model has good memory characteristics and is
suitable for predicting stock time series data, it lacks the ability
to explain final decisions obtained by the model as well as being
affected by human factors. Therefore, this study combines particle
swarm optimization algorithm with LSTM model to take optimal
position of particles as optimal parameters for LSTM neural network
prediction of stock data in order to improve accuracy.

Related work

In the process of designing the stock forecasting model, the stock
market structure is complicated and easy to be affected by external factors, so the stock data has some non-linear and unstable.
Therefore, time series model is the most commonly used method
in stock forecasting. However, due to the high level of noise and
nonlinearity inherent in financial time series data, it is challenging
to accurately predict stock price trends using linear forecasting
models. With the rapid development of computer science and
technology and the increasing maturity of deep learning research,
neural network has become a more effective and accurate method
for stock prediction based on machine learning, especially in China

rules from data and use those rules to predict unknown data. In
the era of big data, neural networks, support vector machines, decision trees and other methods are widely used in the field of stock

1.2

Motivation and contribution

The network parameters of the LSTM model usually depend on the
selection of human experience, and the number of nodes in different
hidden layers and the learning rate will affect the final fitting effect
of the network [5]. Therefore, in this study, PSO algorithm was used
to optimize the number of hidden layer neurons and dropout rate
in LSTM network, so as to reduce the negative impact of parameter
selection on the network. The parameters of LSTM model were
optimized by PSO, and the mean square error MSE and the square
sum of weights MSW were used as the objective functions of PSO
algorithm to evaluate the fitness of each particle. By updating
particle velocity and position, the optimal number of hidden layer
neurons and dropout rate can be found for the network prediction
of the next layer.
The main innovations and contributions of this work include:
(1) Innovative application of hybrid model: By combining particle swarm optimization (PSO) algorithm with Long Short-Term
Memory (LSTM) neural network, this study proposes a new hybrid
model for stock index prediction. This model combines the advantages of two powerful computational tools, namely PSO’s ability for
global optimization and LSTM’s ability for processing time series
data.
(2) Improvement of parameter optimization strategy: Traditionally, the setting of neural network parameters such as the number
of neurons often relies on empirical Settings or tedious manual
adjustments. In this study, the PSO algorithm is used to automatically find the optimal parameter combination, which reduces the
dependence of the model on manual experience and may find a
more accurate model configuration.
(3) Enhance prediction accuracy: The mean square error (MSE)
and the sum of squares of weights are taken as optimization objectives, and the network parameters are iteratively updated to
promote the model to gradually improve its prediction performance
in the course of multiple training, which is expected to improve the
accuracy of stock index prediction.

Literature review

Time series model is the most commonly used method in stock
forecasting, and it is also one of the commonly used tools to predict
stock market changes. Zhang and Sun established the ARIMA
model to forecast and analyze the Shanghai Composite Index of
stocks in the future and found that the model had the best prediction
effect in the next four trading days [6]. Liu and Zhang utilized an
ARIMA model to test and predict the historical closing price data
of selected financial market stocks over a period of two days. They
concluded that the maximum discrepancy between the predicted
values and actual values for the subsequent three days was no more
than 0.04 [7]. KORS and KARAN found that GARCH model has
strong predictive performance [8]. MIAH , RAHMAN et al. used
Akaike and Bayes criteria to prove that GARCH model is superior

124

to other models in predicting return series volatility [9]. Fang et
al. used the ARMI-GARCH model to study media stock prices and
found that the model was more accurate in predicting individual
stocks with “peak and fat tail” distribution characteristics than the
traditional ARIMA model and GARCH model [10]. The study of
Yang and Cao further demonstrated that the prediction error of
the ARMI-GARCH would increase with increase of the number
of prediction steps, and the accuracy would also get lower and
lower [11]. Xu and Liang established the ARMI-GARCH model to
carry out the pseudo-merger of the closing price series of Yutong
Bus through empirical error analysis, and proved that the model
can be applied to the prediction of stock closing price [12]. Xiong
and Che brought volatility into the ARMI-Garch model, built the
ARMI-GarCH-M model, and proved through experiments that the
model would be more accurate in fitting and predicting short-term
stock market prices [13].
Neural network modeling is based on the internal relationship
of data itself, and has strong self-organization, adaptability, robust
learning ability and anti-interference ability. The convergence
speed of neural network to extract economic activity knowledge
from historical data for stock market analysis exceeds the limitation
of traditional quantitative forecasting methods, and provides a new
method for stock market modeling and fore-casting. Huang et al.
used SVM, BP neural network and wavelet neural network models
to train and predict stocks [14]. However, their experiments show
that direct use of pre-dictions based solely on the BP algorithm leads
to unstable returns, slow convergence, and prone to local minima.
Therefore, improvements are needed in terms of accuracy and speed.
Zou used particle swarm optimization (PSO) algorithm to optimize
the BP neural network model [15]. Experimental verification shows
that PSO-BP model is more efficient and feasible for stock market
prediction than other methods. Zhang and Zhang compared BP
neural network and ARMA-GARCH model and found that BP neural
network showed higher accuracy in long-term prediction [16]. Yang
and Chen proposed that the combination of wavelet neural network
(WNN) and ARIMA, an auto-regressive integral moving average
model, is more accurate in short-term stock prediction [17]. Ding
(2021) compared the prediction of stock price trend by SRIMA model
and LSTM model, and concluded that the prediction accuracy based
on LSTM model was better through empirical research [18].
Since stock data is characterized by large amount of data, fuzzy
information, long memory, nonlinear and non-stationary, and deep
learning model has stronger learning ability and adaptive ability,
it can better predict and analyze nonlinear system by using deep
learning model. LSTM adds input gate, output gate, forget gate
on the basis of re-current neural network, which can effectively
deal with data forgetting and gradient ex-plosion problems. It
has good selectivity, memorability and timing internal influence
characteristics, and is very suitable for the prediction of stock price
time series [19]. FISCHER and KRAUSS also believed that whether
predicting stock prices or stock returns, model prediction effect
based on LSTM neural network is better [20]. Analysis of Xiong
and Zhao shows that LSTM model is more suitable for this field of
financial market prediction, and beats other prediction models by
obvious advantages [21].
Although LSTM is a powerful forecasting tool, its network still
have some short-comings. First, LSTM lacks an explanatory model

3 Terminology introduction
3.1 Neural network
I Neural network is a kind of arithmetic model, which is composed
of neuron nodes connected with each other. Each neuron is a computing unit, and each connection be-tween two neurons represents
the weight associated with it.
Neural network is divided into Feed forward Neural Network
(FNN) and feedback neural network. FNN is the most basic type
of neural network. The signal propagates one-way from the input
to the output layer, there is no loop, and the information can only
flow forward. This model is suitable for supervised learning of
numerical data, but it cannot be used together with sequential data
and cannot handle image data well.
The output of feedback neural network is not only related to the
current input and the weight of the network, but also related to the
input before the network, which has strong associative memory
ability and optimization computing ability. Common model structures include: Recurrent Neural Network (RNN), LSTM, etc. RNN
is the most commonly used feedback neural network, which can
process data with sequence changes. In this way, a bidirectional
cycle can be formed, and time information can be extracted in the
cycle, which has the effect of short-term memory. RNN neurons
can receive information from the previous neuron and the outside,
process it, and then transmit it to the next neuron, which makes
it widely used in time series tasks. Figure 1 shows the network
structure of RNN.
By combining time series data with neural network, RNN can
retain the influence of previous data on the present. However, with
the increase of time series, it is found that RNN is weak in processing
long time series. Moreover, with the increase of time series and

125

depth of model, it is impossible to avoid the problems of gradient
disappearing and gradient explosion when calculating gradient,
which need to be improved [23]. LSTM can solve these problems.
It designed neurons of each layer into a structure with multiple
“gates”, so that the error can be directly and intact transmitted to
the next layer in the process of propagation without completely
disappearing, so it has good convergence [24].

Particle Swarm Optimization Algorithm

Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO), which originated from the
study of bird predation behavior, is a global search algorithm for
optimal values. The optimization process of PSO algorithm can
be compared to the foraging process of birds. Each individual in
the flock is equivalent to each particle in the swarm, and the food
sought by the birds is the optimal solution sought by the particles
[25].
Suppose that in a D-dimensional search space, there are N
particles in a particle swarm, each of which represents a potential solution. The position of particle in D dimensional space is
(Xi1, Xi2, . . . . . . , Xid ), the speed is(Vi1, Vi2, . . . . . . , Vid ), the optimal
position is (Pi1, Pi2, . . . . . . , Pid ), that is, the global optimal value is
P gbest = (Pg1, Pg2, . . . . . . , Pgd ). The core of PSO algorithm is to
make particles gradually approach the optimal position by constantly adjusting the speed and position of particles. The update of
particle swarm in position and speed can be expressed as formula

ESTABLISH PSO-LSTM MODEL

The LSTM model is suitable for processing time series data, but it
is difficult to determine parameters such as the number of neurons
in the network. In general, it is based on experience and has a
great deal of randomness. PSO algorithm is used to optimize the
number of neurons and the learning rate of LSTM neural network
to improve the prediction accuracy of LSTM model. By using the
PSO algorithm, the position information of the optimal individual
is used as the initial neural number, output rate and batch size
of LSTM to train the LSTM neural network, so as to improve the
prediction accuracy of the LSTM model [27].
Figure 3 shows the structure of the PSO-LSTM model, which
uses the PSO algorithm to search the parameters of LSTM.
(1) Initialize PSO parameters to determine particle swarm size,
iteration number, search dimension, initial inertia weight and learning factor.
(2) Set the position and speed of the particle. Generate a random
particle Xi=(h1,h2,d,b), h1 and h2 represent the number of neurons
at layer 1 and Layer 2 of the neural network, d is the dropout ratio,

For each particle, the current fitness value is compared with the
historical optimal fitness value, and the individual optimal position
is updated. Compare the fitness values of all particles and update
the global optimal position.
According to the speed update formula of PSO algorithm, the
speed and position of particles are adjusted.
(5) Iteration. Calculate the globally optimal particle position.
The global optimal particle position was mapped to the number of
neurons, dropout rate and batch-size of LSTM neural network, and
the optimal parameters were obtained.
(6) Make predictions. The LSTM neural network is trained by
using the global optimal particle position to determine the optimal
parameters, and the final prediction results are output.

Conclusion

This study realizes a model that can be used in quantitative investment practice teaching to forecast stocks. The model is based on
LSTM neural network to forecast stock returns. In the process of
model study, to solve the problem that the prediction results of
LSTM neural network are easy to be affected by network structural
parameters, researchers chose PSO to optimize the parameters of
the network. The prediction effect of LSTM model optimized based
on PSO algorithm is good, which has certain research value and
practicability. The stock market itself is a complex system, which is
affected by many factors such as national political situation, policy,
finance and enterprise situation. The stock index prediction based
on PSO-LSTM algorithm is only a data prediction from a technical
perspective. Therefore, when the external environment changes
greatly, such as changes in enterprise conditions, there will often be
relatively large deviations when making predictions based on past
data. Therefore, when making stock investment, investors should
not only refer to the results of data model analysis, but also consider the influence of external economic, policy, social and cultural
factors.

weights of the actual and tested values of the training set is used as
the objective function of the PSO, where the value of 𝛾 is set to 0.9.
Calculate the position and fitness values of the particle in each
iteration. Global optimal particle position was mapped into the
number of neurons, dropout rate and batch-size of the LSTM, and
the LSTM neural network is trained with the optimal parameters
and the final prediction results are obtained.
Model training was carried out according to the above steps, the
test set was input into the model, and the prediction results were
output, as shown in Figure 8. The green solid line is the predicted
value, and red line is the true value. The predicted stock return is
in line with the trend of the true value. The MAE is 0.046087, the
MSE is 0.09029, and the RMSE is 0.095022. The error value is relatively small, and the results show that the LSTM prediction model
optimized by PSO algorithm has a better fitting degree, which is
consistent with the conclusion proposed by Xie et al. [28]. Because
the stock market is greatly affected by external factors, so from
the technical point of view, the accuracy of prediction is relatively
high.
